VoiceGate

Require members to join a specific voice channel before they can send text messages. Keep your server voice-focused when needed, without any extra setup.

Add to Discord
• Instant check: Anyone not in the specific voice channel gets their message deleted.
• Staff Exemptions: Mods and admins can chat freely anywhere.
• Simple Admin Controls: Use /gate enable and /gate disable to toggle it on or off.
